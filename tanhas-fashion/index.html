Elegance in Every Stitch

Our story

A label built on a simple promise

Tanha’s Fashion is a women’s wear label built around one idea: elegant wear for every woman. Premium, modern and timeless — the three words we use to describe every piece we put our name to.

Our collections follow the season. Lightweight lawn and soft cottons through the summer months; printed linen, marina and embroidered suits when the weather turns. Everything is ready to wear, so nothing sits in your wardrobe waiting to be stitched.

What we make
Ready-to-wear eastern wear — two- and three-piece suits, coordinated sets, kurtis and long frocks.
Fabrics
Lawn, linen, marina, sheesha silk and chiffon.
Sizes
Small to Large, and XS to XL depending on the piece. Each product lists the sizes it is actually available in.
How to order
Message us on Instagram. We confirm availability, price and delivery with you directly.
